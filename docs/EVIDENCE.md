## Surgical thresholds (largest of sinus, junction and ascending diameters)

| Cause | ACC/AHA 2022 | ESC 2024 |
|---|---|---|
| Tricuspid valve, sporadic | Recommended ≥ 55 mm or confirmed rapid growth; reasonable ≥ 50 mm with an experienced team, area/height ≥ 10 cm²/m when height is > 1 SD from average, ≥ 45 mm with aortic valve surgery | Recommended ≥ 55 mm; should be considered > 52 mm at low surgical risk, ≥ 45 mm (low risk) or ≥ 50 mm with aortic valve surgery, ≥ 50 mm with other cardiac surgery |
| Bicuspid aortic valve | Recommended ≥ 55 mm; reasonable with area/height ≥ 10 cm²/m, 50–54 mm with a risk factor, ≥ 45 mm with aortic valve surgery; may be reasonable 50–54 mm at low risk; before pregnancy ≥ 50 mm | Recommended ≥ 55 mm and ≥ 50 mm for the root phenotype; should be considered ≥ 50 mm for the ascending phenotype with risk factors, ≥ 45 mm with aortic valve surgery |
| Marfan syndrome | Recommended ≥ 50 mm; reasonable ≥ 45 mm with high-risk features or area/height ≥ 10 cm²/m; before pregnancy > 45 mm | Recommended ≥ 50 mm; should be considered ≥ 45 mm with high-risk features; before pregnancy > 45 mm |
| Loeys–Dietz syndrome | Gene-specific, not encoded | Should be considered ≥ 45 mm, or ≥ 40 mm with high-risk features |
| Heritable, no gene identified | Recommended ≥ 50 mm, or ≥ 45 mm with high-risk features | Not encoded |

Indexed thresholds (ACC/AHA 2022): aortic size index ≥ 3.08 cm/m², aortic height index ≥ 3.21 cm/m, cross-sectional area/height ≥ 10 cm²/m. Status: checked for the main thresholds; some ESC details (Marfan high-risk threshold, the exact risk-factor list for the bicuspid ascending phenotype) come from secondary sources. Not encoded: Turner syndrome, vascular Ehlers–Danlos syndrome.

## Growth (2022 ACC/AHA)

Sporadic aneurysm: ≥ 5 mm in 1 year, or ≥ 3 mm/year across 2 consecutive years. Bicuspid valve or heritable disease: ≥ 3 mm in 1 year. The guideline requires confirmed growth; the viewer compares studies done with the same method and flags changes within measurement variability.
